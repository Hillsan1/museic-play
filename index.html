How to Play

  1. Draw. Read a card out loud.
  2. Pick. Everyone finds a song that fits. 45 seconds. Cards marked 90 SEC get 90.
  3. Play. Play 30 seconds of each song on the speaker.
  4. Rate. Everyone else scores your song 1 to 10. Never score your own.
  5. Win. The highest total wins the round: +10 points. First to 50 wins!

Tie?

Only the tied players pick new songs. Everyone else votes again.

Top 5 cards

Everyone builds a list of five songs and plays a short snippet of each. Best list wins the round.
